what am I looking at?

The stars asterism named in the photo, each at the distance its parallax gives. The photo hangs in space where Earth's view of them was, and the lines from Earth pass through it at each star's place in the picture.

Earth and the Sun are not to scale: from out here they would be far too small to see. The other stars are the catalog's, with their brightness turned up as the camera backs away.
