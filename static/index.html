asterism

Upload a photo of the night sky — get back a labelled map of what you were looking at.

What is this?

Upload a photo of the night sky and asterism tells you what you were looking at: it names the stars, draws the constellations, and marks the Moon and planets in your frame.

It works out where your camera was pointing from the star pattern alone — no location, no compass, no account.

Works best with an ordinary phone photo of the night sky. Handheld and a little hazy is fine; the more stars you can see, the better it does.

Uploads are public, and deleted after 24 hours.

Anyone with the result link can view your photo and its labels, and successful solves appear in the "recently solved" feed on this page. GPS metadata is removed from the stored photo (uploads with location data we can't remove are rejected), coordinates shown in results are rounded to city scale, and everything is deleted after 24 hours — apart from the occasional solve kept on as an example of what the site does.
